Fill in what people should see. You get a link to your own card page and the same card drawn for a 2.9″ e‑paper screen. Everything lives inside the link, so nothing is stored on a server.

Put it on the screen

Flash the firmware once, plug the ESP32 in over USB and press Send. Works in Chrome and Edge on a computer.

How it works

  1. Fill in your card. Name, role, links. The preview shows exactly what the e‑ink screen will draw, pixel for pixel.
  2. Share the link. It opens your card page with a Save contact button. The QR code on the screen opens the same page.
  3. Send it to the screen. An ESP32 with a 2.9″ Waveshare panel keeps the image in flash. E‑ink holds it with the power off.
